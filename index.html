The Philosophy

"We didn't want a restaurant you rush through. We wanted a room you lose track of time in."

Fursat Kitchen & Bar takes the grammar of the South Indian table — banana leaf, filter kaapi, ferment and spice — and resets it for a Madhapur evening. Same techniques, unrushed room. Dishes are built for sharing, drinks for lingering.
